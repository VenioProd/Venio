IVProtecting your business

Your gallery never has to stop because of us.

Five written commitments.

01

You hold your own data

How it worksYou can retrieve everything, at any time. Every month, a complete copy is delivered to you.

  • Artworks, contacts, documents, invoices
  • Formats readable by other software
  • Full-resolution images
02

The software is held in escrow

How it worksThe complete software and its operating instructions are held by a trusted third party. If Venio ceases to exist, they are released to you.

  • Written into the contract
  • Another provider can take over
  • Within one week
03

Backups are tested

How it worksA backup every night, in France. Every month, we check that it can be restored and send you the results.

  • Checked every month
  • Backups kept for 90 days
  • Report sent to you
04

Access is controlled

How it worksOne account per person, secure sign-in and permissions based on roles. Every time a contract is opened, it is recorded.

  • History kept for twelve months
  • Artist prices are visible only to authorised users
  • Access to sensitive documents is restricted by role
05

Continuity is planned

How it worksA document explains how to run the software without us. We review it together once a year.

  • Written operating instructions
  • You hold emergency access
  • Reviewed every year
