Margit Holzner

The Rediscovered Line — Drawings, 1950–1975

5 September – 19 December 2026 · Maison Vallier, upper gallery

Nearly fifty sheets, most previously unseen, trace three decades of Margit Holzner’s drawing practice: studio charcoals, studies of reclining figures and sketchbooks drawn from life in Montparnasse and later Vienna. They reveal a hand that searches, revisits and erases, never giving way to anecdote.

The exhibition brings together works held in private collections since their direct acquisition from the artist, shown outside the studio for the first time.

Publications and catalogues raisonnés

Maison Vallier publishes and updates the catalogues raisonnés of the artists whose work it oversees, working with family archives and the relevant expert committees. Every painting offered for sale undergoes an examination of its stretcher, canvas reverse and exhibition labels, key evidence of its provenance.

This examination, carried out in storage before any gallery presentation, accompanies every artwork dossier provided to buyers: ownership history, exhibitions and bibliography.

  • Catalogue raisonné Wladimir Sorel, 1946–1969 Maison Vallier Publishing, 2011 — 2 volumes, 640 entries
  • Bronisław Ackerman, Painting the Reconstruction Monograph, Maison Vallier Publishing, 2019
  • Margit Holzner, Drawing as a Diary Exhibition catalogue, 2026, 96 pages

The gallery

Founded in 1954 by Antoine Vallier, the gallery is now directed by his granddaughter, Camille Vallier. Three generations sharing a perspective on post-war painting, the School of Paris and twentieth-century master drawings.
