The gallery

Emerging artists,
first exhibitions

Studio Ravel opened in 2021 in Marseille’s 2nd arrondissement, a short walk from Cours Julien. The gallery gives emerging artists their first solo exhibition, alongside an editions programme designed to make collecting accessible.

Studio Ravel participates in Art Brussels and Paris Internationale, supporting its artists over time: first commissions, first catalogue texts and first acquisitions by public collections.
